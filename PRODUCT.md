# PRODUCT VISION & PHILOSOPHY

## Vision
The project is a prototype of a **Living Drawing Workshop**. 
It aims to allow artists and beginners to bring their drawings to life easily by turning them into interactive entities with states, behaviors, and rules.

## Philosophy
> **Bringing a drawing to life should be as fun and accessible as drawing.**
The project should not become a complex game engine (like Unity, Spline, Rive). Creation must remain visual, direct, and playful.

## Target Audience
Artists, illustrators, beginners, and storytellers who want to create small interactive scenes, narratives, or playful toys without learning complex programming languages.

## Core Concepts
- **Entities**: Objects on the canvas containing drawings, properties, states, and tags.
- **Drawing as primitive**: Using custom drawn shapes as the core interactive elements.
- **States**: Visual representations of different situations (e.g., Seed, Sprout, Flower).
- **Transitions**: Interpolation between states.
- **Rule Book**: A natural language-like system (WHEN/THEN) to define interactions.
- **Systemic Interactions**: Combining simple properties to create emergent behaviors.

## Inspirations
Sok Stories, Figma, Julian's Editor, Spline, Rive, PenPen, Flow Aura, Unity Playmaker, Dreams, Scribblenauts Unlimited, Powder Game, Prosopopus.

## Product Limits
- NOT a general-purpose simulation engine.
- NOT a node-based complex logic graph.
- Keep the physics 2D simple for emergent interactions, without becoming a rigid body simulation tool.
